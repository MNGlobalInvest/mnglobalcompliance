Who it is for

Built for HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, and remodeling owners.

If you answer the calls, build the quotes, and send the invoices yourself, this is for you. Most owners in your position recognize at least one of these:

Missed after-hours calls

Leads ring once, get voicemail, and call the next contractor on the list.

Quotes typed in Word

Every quote starts from scratch, and follow-up depends on your memory.

Invoices sent monthly

Finished jobs sit unbilled for weeks, and cash arrives later than it should.

Receipts in a box

Paper piles up until someone has to sort it by hand.

Best fit: owner-operated businesses with roughly two to 25 people, where the owner is the buyer. It is not a fit for franchises or companies with a compliance department.

The audit

One written blueprint, from lead to cash.

You answer a short questionnaire in writing. We map how work moves in your business today and deliver the results as a document you can hand to anyone on your team.

  1. LeadCall, text, form
  2. QuoteEstimate sent
  3. JobScheduled, done
  4. InvoiceBilled
  5. CashCollected

What the blueprint contains

  • A current-state map from lead to cash
  • Your top three bottlenecks, with evidence from your answers
  • Estimates of hours and cash effects, each with its inputs shown
  • Tools with costs, sorted into do now, later, and skip
  • A 30-day rollout plan

What we do not do

  • Bookkeeping, tax, or legal work
  • Take passwords or log in to your accounts
  • Guarantee outcomes or results
  • Share findings before the audit fee is paid

Honest about our estimates: every number in your blueprint shows the inputs behind it and is labeled an estimate. Anything you did not provide is marked as an assumption.

How it works

Seven days, all in writing.

  1. Day 0. Agreement signed and payment received. You get the start date and delivery date in writing.
  2. Day 1. We read your questionnaire answers and list any gaps. Missing items are requested as screenshots or exports, never passwords.
  3. Days 2 to 3. We map your current state from lead to cash.
  4. Day 4. We rank your bottlenecks and build estimates with the inputs shown.
  5. Day 5. We choose tools and costs, sort them into do now, later, and skip, and write the 30-day rollout.
  6. Day 6. The founder reviews the full packet.
  7. Day 7. The blueprint is delivered in writing with a cover note.
  8. Days 8 to 14. Optional proposal window. The audit credit applies if you sign a build by day 14.
